## Primary public references to prioritize

- Final official trailers, clips, and released film frames
- Official production notes and behind-the-scenes material
- Wētā Workshop concept-development pages, labeled as concept rather than final geometry
- Interviews with the final puppet, creature, VFX, and animatronic teams
- The novel for lore, always labeled separately from the movie design

## Measurement rule

No dimension becomes “confirmed” from a single image. Record source, timestamp/page, object used for scale, relative depth, perspective correction, independent views, CAD residual, uncertainty, and confidence.
